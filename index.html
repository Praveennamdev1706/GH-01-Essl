Active business rules for deduction calculation
✅ On Time
≤ 10:05 AM
Employee is marked on time. No deduction.
Deduction: 0
🕐 Late Window
10:06 AM – 10:20 AM
First 4 arrivals between 10:06 AM and 10:20 AM per payroll cycle are free. From the 5th occurrence onward, deduct 0.25 day per occurrence.
– 0.25 day (after 4 free)
🔴 Hard Late
After 10:20 AM
Arrivals after 10:20 AM and up to 12:00 PM incur a direct 0.25-day deduction, regardless of the four free late arrivals.
– 0.25 day (after 4 free)
🏃 Early Out
Out before 6:30 PM
Employee left before the official closing time. Immediate deduction regardless of total hours worked.
– 0.25 day (immediate)
⏱ Short Leave (Removed)
No separate short-leave window
Short-leave deductions are disabled. Late arrivals are handled by the late-arrival rule.
No separate deduction
🌓 Half Day
Arrival after 12:00 PM
Arrival after 12:00 PM, including after 2:15 PM, results in a 0.5-day deduction.
– 0.50 day
📅 Full Day
Missing > 4 hrs or Absent
Employee missed more than 4 hours of the shift, or was fully absent (no punch-in/out).
– 1.00 day
📆 Weekly Off
All Sundays + 2nd & 4th Saturday
All Sundays and the 2nd & 4th Saturdays of each month are designated Weekly Off. 1st, 3rd, 5th Saturdays are working.
No Deduction
